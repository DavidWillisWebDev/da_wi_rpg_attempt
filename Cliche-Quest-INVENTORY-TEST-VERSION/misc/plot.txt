Extremely clueless about the fact that the kingdom's princess was captured by a stupidly-named villain. King Useless Royal tasks the hero with... Buying groceries from Definitely Not Shady. The Hero enters the island of Plaht Tevaice. It's a jungle island.

There's a guard from Intarou present. He's an idiot with the braincells of a doorknob. Actually, even a doorknob is smarter than him.

At some point, the Hero encounters another hero [player's choice for name]. Player 3 becomes a party member.

At a later point, the Hero encounters a hero [you know the idea on names by now]. Player 4 becomes a party member.
