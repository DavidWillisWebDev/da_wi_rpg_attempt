Idea #1: class system
  Concept:
    A class system, similar to games like Fire Emblem or Final Fantasy.
    Different classes have different effects on the gameplay. (mages have higher magic power, but less physical power, brawlers have higher physical power, but less magic power, etc.)
    Classes also have unique abilities exclusive to said class.

  Notes:
    Will need a new attribute in the character constructor, called "class".
    character.class will be a string property. It will initially start as none ("").
